Written by two members of JetBrains' Kotlin team plus the language's original authors, this is the closest thing to a definitive reference on how Kotlin is meant to be used. The second edition's overhaul of the coroutines chapters is what keeps it near the top of this list — most competing books still treat async Kotlin as an afterthought.
